Sponsorship Opportunities

Highlander Robotics provides real-life STEM experiences through regional and national robotics competitions. As a community-based team, we do not receive any funding from the school. Our team of student innovators relies on the support of volunteers and sponsors to help offset expenses and provide the best program possible.

Benefits of Supporting Highlander Robotics

  • Our Mission - We started the first FRC team in our community!
  • Advertising - Your corporate branding will be displayed on everything we wear - including our robot.
  • FIRST Robotics students are: 4x as likely to pursue a career in engineering.
  • Supporting STEM - Investment in the team is an investment in future STEM leaders and innovators.
  • We made it to Worlds in Houston, Texas in 2022 and 2023!
  • Tax Benefits - We have 501(c)(3) status under Piedmont Makers, so donations to the team are tax deductible as a non-profit.
  • Not only does participation on the team build technical skills, it also builds leadership, business, and communication skills, key abilities for future success: FIRST Robotics students are 2x as likely to volunteer in their communities.
  • Visibility for your company at our regional and national competitions where thousands of people attend high-level robotics competitions.
