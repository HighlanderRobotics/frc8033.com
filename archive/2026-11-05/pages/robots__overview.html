ROBOTS

Take a walk through a gallery of Team 8033's creations.

2020

Nessie

Team 8033's Inaugural Robot was designed, constructed, and tested in 2019 by our founders. Although it was unable to compete due to the COVID-19 lockdown, our team bonded in the process of making it. It features a low cargo shooter, a dual-piston extending intake, and a control panel with acolor sensor underneath.

2021

Whirlpool

Highlander Robotics' second robot, at a size of 29x29x26 inches and at a weight of 110 pounds, Whirlpool sports a Swerve drive, a hood-style shooter, a cable-track climber, and a circular, revolving routing mechanism capable of carrying 5 balls.

More Information

2022

Selkie

Highlander Robotics's 2022 robot has a small frame fitted with a simplistic square swerve drive, a dual-purpose router/shooter subassembly capable of holding 2 balls, an over-bumper intake, and a two-stage climber.

More Information

2023

Paddo

Paddo, our latest robot, is equipped with a 28" by 30" swerve drive, a diagonal superstructure, a 3-stage cascading elevator, a twin-counterspun roller grabber, an over-the-bumper intake, and a belt-based routing system.
