Outreach 2020-2021

Maker Faire

05/2021

At the annual Maker Faire, hosted by Piedmont Makers, Highlander team members held an open house of their workshop for community members and demonstrated their robot to kids and adults.

PUSD Board Meeting

07/2020

Team members attended a Piedmont Unified School District Board Meeting. Highlander team members talked to the School Board about the FIRST Robotics Competition program and the tools the fundraiser would provide. All of this paved the way for the lab that we now get to use.

Virtual Open House

04/2020

Our team held a Robotics Virtual Open House, where team members talked about the FIRST Robotics Competition program to recruit new members.
