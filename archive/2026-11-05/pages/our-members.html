Cassie Colby

Captain

I am a Senior at Piedmont High School and have been coding for about 8 years. In middle school I did FLL and FTC. After joining 8033, I led Lovat in my freshman and sophmore year. For the last 4 years I have mentored local FTC teams, and done strategy for the team. Additionally, this is my second year of drive coaching. I'm looking forward to a great season of improving our robot creation process and improving the community.
