Banshee

2024  Crescendo Robot

Our 2024 robot, Banshee, was the most complex and challenging robot we’ve built so far. Banshee lead us to a finalist at SFR and to the World Championships in Houston and all the way to the Milstein Divistion finals.  

Drivetrain & Super-
structure

Drivetrain

  • 27.5” by 32” to accommodate under the bumper intake
  • 27x27 MK4i swerve modules with Krakens & L3 gearing

Superstructure

  • Riveted gusset and tubing construction

Elevator + Carriage

  • Large central compliant wheel roller surrounded by smaller silicone-covered dead axle rollers, all actively driven
  • These rollers guide the ring smoothly along a downward curved path into the amp.
  • Mounted on 2 stage cascading elevator to reach amp

Climber

  • Two polycarbonate arms with a CF bar attached to a MAXSpline
  • Deployed via torsion springs, winched in via strap on a powered spool

Shooter

  • Chain driven pivoting with rollers for routing
  • Co-axial roller-MaxSpline allows for high torsional stiffness despite being driven from one side
  • Independently powered left and right flywheels to create spin
