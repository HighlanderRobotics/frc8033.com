Wisp

2026 Rebuilt Robot

Our 2026 robot, Wisp, was the most complex and challenging robot we’ve ever built and went through several iterations as the season progressed. Wisp put up a performance of all time, taking us to the NorCal District Championship.

Drivetrain & Electronics

Drivetrain

  • 27.5"x27.5" for incresed ball capacity
  • MK5n swerve modules with Krakens (X60 drive and X44 Steer) & R1 gearing

Electrical

  • Brainpan and upside down electronics for easy access

Intake

Intake

  • Coaxial power for rollers
  • SRPP arms for durability

Climber

  • Tube in tube climber with dynema chord to retract
  • Powered by Kraken X60 on a MaxPlanetary gearbox
  • Locking stage in MaxPlanetary to hold in place

Spindexer

  • Single Piece turret mounting
  • Powered by Kraken x60

Turret

  • Custon 10DP gear to power rotation
  • 265 degres of motion
  • CRT to track location

Shooter

  • Adjustable hood with 3D printed nylon gear racks
  • Dual x60 powered flywheel
  • Brass Flywheel for higher throughput
